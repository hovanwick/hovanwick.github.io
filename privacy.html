Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

FlowKit ("the app", "we") is a personal, self-hosted tool run by its owner on the owner's own computer. It publishes videos the owner creates to the owner's own TikTok account, YouTube channel and Facebook Page. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and no one other than the owner connects an account to it. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

1. Data we access

The app only accesses data after the account owner signs in on the platform's own consent screen and grants the permissions listed below.

TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API)

  • Scopes: user.info.basic, and video.upload or video.publish.
  • Data: the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar), the access token and refresh token, and the list of granted scopes.
  • Content: the video files and captions the owner chooses to post, and the publish status and post ID TikTok returns.

YouTube (YouTube API Services)

  • Scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly.
  • Data: the channel ID, channel title and handle, the OAuth access token and refresh token, and the list of granted scopes.
  • Content: the video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails the owner chooses to upload, and the video ID YouTube returns.

Facebook (Meta Graph API)

  • Permissions: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, business_management.
  • Data: the IDs and names of the Pages the owner manages and their Page access tokens.
  • Content: the video files, titles and descriptions the owner chooses to post, and the video ID Facebook returns.

2. How we use the data

  • To upload and publish videos that the owner created and explicitly approved, to the owner's own accounts.
  • To show the owner which account is connected and the links to the published posts.
  • To keep the connection working by refreshing access tokens.

We do not use the data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training any AI or machine-learning model, and we do not access any other user's data.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and account identifiers are stored only in local files on the owner's computer. They are never uploaded to a server operated by us; the app has no such server. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta), over HTTPS.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. The only recipients are the platforms themselves, when the app calls their APIs on the owner's behalf.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner disconnects an account. Disconnecting revokes the app's access and the local token files are deleted. Step-by-step instructions are on the Data Deletion page. Videos already published stay on the platform until the owner deletes them there.

6. Platform-specific terms

YouTube

FlowKit uses YouTube API Services. By using FlowKit you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke FlowKit's access at any time on the Google security settings page. FlowKit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok

Use of TikTok through FlowKit is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy. You can remove FlowKit's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → apps with access to your account.

Facebook

Use of Facebook through FlowKit is subject to the Meta Terms of Service and the Meta Privacy Policy. You can remove FlowKit under Facebook Settings → Business integrations.

7. Children

FlowKit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
