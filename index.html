Publish the videos I make to my own social accounts

FlowKit is a personal, self-hosted tool. It renders short educational videos on my computer and, after I review and approve each one, posts it to my own TikTok account, YouTube channel and Facebook Page through the platforms' official APIs.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

How it works

1. Create

A vertical 9:16 video is rendered locally from my own script, images and narration.

2. Review

I watch the video, edit the caption and approve it. Nothing is posted without that approval.

3. Publish

FlowKit uploads the approved video to the accounts I connected, then shows me the post links.

Platforms

TikTok

Login Kit and the Content Posting API: uploads a video as a draft to my TikTok inbox, or posts it directly to my profile with the caption and privacy level I chose.

YouTube

YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3): uploads the video to my channel with its title, description, tags and thumbnail.

Facebook

Meta Graph API: publishes the video as a Reel or a video on a Facebook Page I manage.
