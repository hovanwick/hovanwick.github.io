Data Deletion Instructions

FlowKit stores only the tokens and account identifiers needed to publish to accounts you connected, in local files on the owner's computer. Here is how to remove them.

1. Revoke access on the platform

PlatformWhere to revoke
TikTok TikTok app → Profile → menu → Settings and privacy → Security → apps with access to your account → FlowKit → Remove access.
YouTube / Google myaccount.google.com/permissions → FlowKit → Delete all connections.
Facebook Facebook → Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations → FlowKit → Remove.

Revoking makes every token FlowKit holds for that account stop working immediately.

2. Delete the local data

On the computer running FlowKit, delete the token file of the platform from the output/_shared/ folder:

  • tiktok_token.json (TikTok)
  • youtube_token.json (YouTube)
  • facebook_page.json (Facebook)

3. Ask us to delete it

You can also email YOUR_EMAIL@gmail.com with the subject "FlowKit data deletion" and the platform name. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Videos you already published are not removed by these steps. Delete them on TikTok, YouTube or Facebook directly.
